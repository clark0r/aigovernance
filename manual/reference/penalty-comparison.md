## Reading the table

Penalty size alone is a poor proxy for practical risk. Two structural features matter more:
1. **Private right of action** (BIPA, TCPA, FCRA, VPPA-adjacent, Illinois HB 3773, Wiretap Act/SCA) —
   these are litigation-driven regimes independent of any regulator's enforcement priorities, and are
   where the largest real-world settlements have actually landed.
2. **Cure periods sunsetting** — several US state privacy laws (Colorado, Connecticut, Oregon, New
   Hampshire) removed automatic 30/60-day cure rights between 2025-2026; treat "curable" as a
   time-limited assumption, not a standing feature.
